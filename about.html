Anyone can learn the tools for physics research -

the hardest part is knowing what's relevant.

The PHYSDepot Physics Resource Library is built for the curious and innovative - anyone who is looking to learn the skills of physics research, branch between subfields, or learn new tools. In the age of science featuring an intense push to make data and software open-source, our goal is three-fold: to organize, communicate, and build upon physics and STEM "resources" - any open-source datasets, software, simulations, learning tools, packages, websites, platforms, workshops, tutorials, and more. Thusfar, we have acheieved this goal through a combined effort pushed by a broad, online community of students and scientists across the globe.

Help us by contributing your favorite resources here. Together, we can bring all of physics research to your fingertips, one resource at a time.

Goals of PHYSDepot

Our goals at PHYSDepot are simple, yet all-encompassing:

  • To lower the barrier of entry to physics research and mentorship by collecting as many resources and "places to start" in one location.
  • To provide a platform for all scientists - beginner through professional - to acquaint themselves with the breadth of tools created for doing physics regardless of what skills/jargon they may or may not have yet become accustomed to.
  • To create a maintained, intuitive list of these resources and a community of learners to share ideas, open questions, and learning resources.

Part of our efforts, as an organization, a resource, and a community, is to build an educational, academic, and informative platform that grows parallel to academia. We look to formalize education in "tacit" knowledge of scientific research: laboratory skills, communication skills, adjacent (yet pertinent) "specialized" knowledge, and topics on the furthest edge of scientific innovation. Through our frequent workshops, online talks, and virtual reading/literature groups, we are paving the way to achieving this goal and formalizing a core curriculum.

Code of Conduct

All users of PHYSDepot, including contributors, must use this website professionally and interact with other contributors kindly. We have a zero-tolerance policy for any of the following:

  • Use of our platform for harassment or belittlement of contributors or users. This platform serves as a welcoming environment for all willing to learn regardless of race, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual identity, national origin, disability, religion, or other background.
  • Use of our platform for any legally or sexually explicit purposes.

Users and contributors may notify us of potential abuses by emailing a lead contributor at amelia@physdepot.org or ricky@physdepot.org.

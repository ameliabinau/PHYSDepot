Oct 13
Literature Review

Hubble's 1929 Radial Velocity Scavenger Hunt

6:00PM EST • Online (Discord)

A review of Hubble's 1929 paper, "A Relation Between Distance and Radial Velocity Among Extra-Galactic Nebulae". We review this paper as a form of a "scavenger hunt", where we collectively attempt to identify the surface-level problems with the paper in a general sense. Then we examine the deeper problems and revere in the fact that Hubble still managed to retrieve a fundamental cosmological result despite these errors.
